## Data freshness

NEISS data is released by CPSC on an annual cycle; a new year of data typically becomes available
partway through the following year. This app determines the latest available year by checking
CPSC's own archive listing at the time the data pipeline runs -- it is never assumed to be the
current calendar year. The specific years currently loaded, and when the latest year was detected,
are shown in the live pipeline metadata below, all read from the pipeline's own committed output
rather than restated here.
